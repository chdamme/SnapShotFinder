SnapShotFinder · Help & privacy

From photo to location

Open or drop photos. SnapShotFinder reads their embedded EXIF GPS data, numbers the files in natural filename order, and places matching markers on two Geoportail maps. Select a row or a marker to view that photo. Arrow keys move between photos.

No GPS data?

A photo can still be previewed without GPS data. The program does not guess its location or analyse landmarks. Enable location tagging in your camera before taking a photo. Use original files: messaging apps, image editing or screenshots may remove metadata.

Supported files

The built-in reader handles standard EXIF metadata in JPEG, PNG, TIFF and WebP. HEIC/HEIF, AVIF, RAW, BigTIFF and XMP-only location data are not read in this version. Export such files to JPEG with metadata preserved. TIFF preview depends on your browser; coordinates can still be read when a preview is unavailable.

Coordinates and addresses

Latitude and longitude are WGS84. X/Y LUREF are metres in EPSG:2169, calculated when the Geoportail projection library is available and only within the Luxembourg region. Decimal places are display formatting, not an accuracy guarantee. “Direction” is the camera's EXIF compass heading; T means true north and M means magnetic north.

An address result is the nearest known address, not proof that the camera was at that property. Its distance is shown separately. Luxembourg reverse geocoding is skipped far outside the region. A numbered marker is the camera location, not necessarily the location of the photographed object. A group-centre cross is only a map reference.

Privacy and internet access

Your photo files are read locally and are not uploaded by this application. Geoportail receives map requests and GPS coordinates for address searches. Untick “Find nearest addresses” before opening photos to stop those lookups; map requests still reveal the viewed area. Clearing the session removes its photos and address cache from this page.

Photo reading and preview work without a connection. Maps, LUREF conversion and address lookup need Geoportail. Geoportail's API documentation requires ACT access approval for a hosting domain. A blank or denied map can therefore be a domain-permission, network or provider issue.

One replacement file

All application code and styling are inside index.html. Your old java.js and stylesheet.css are no longer loaded. Geoportail supplies its mapping libraries online. CSV exports include all selected files, even those without GPS; a search filter does not limit the export.
